About

What SciVigilance is

A daily signal on space and science. One brand across the website, the weekly newsletter, YouTube, TikTok and X (Science in 280, @SciVigil).

The mission

SciVigilance gathers the latest real space, science and AI news in one place, with every story linked to its original source, so you can see what happened and read the full report from the people who published it.

The news feed is assembled automatically from public RSS feeds of science publishers and agencies. Each card shows the source's headline, a short excerpt from the source's own summary (trimmed to about 220 characters) and the source's image. We don't rewrite or add to those summaries, and stories without a real link and image are skipped.

One brand, every channel

SciVigilance is one brand with several channels. The website, the newsletter, the YouTube channel, TikTok and our X account (Science in 280, @SciVigil) work as one and link to each other:

  • Website: science, space and AI news refreshed four times a day, plus our X posts, featured X accounts and Shorts, each linked to its source.
  • X: Science in 280 (@SciVigil): daily science news in 280 characters or fewer.
  • YouTube (@ciberaifacts) and TikTok (@ciber362): short science videos made from real footage.
  • Newsletter: the week's top stories, free in your inbox every Sunday morning.

Where the content comes from

Science news: RSS feeds from NASA, ESA, Space.com, Phys.org, ScienceDaily, Quanta Magazine, Science News, New Scientist, Live Science, ScienceAlert, Universe Today and MIT News. Stories from the last 7 days are kept, up to 8 per source and 60 in total, with duplicates removed and sources mixed so no single outlet dominates.

AI news: RSS feeds from Hugging Face, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Google AI, Simon Willison, Ars Technica, The Verge, MIT News, MIT Technology Review, MarkTechPost and The Decoder. A keyword filter keeps posts about models, releases and benchmarks and drops funding, lawsuit and business stories. Up to 30 stories from the last 14 days are kept.

X posts: our own posts from Science in 280 (@SciVigil), plus original posts from space agencies and launch companies and Elon Musk's tech, AI and science posts. Replies, reposts, promotions and political posts are filtered out.

01

Always linked to the source

Every story, post and video credit points to where it came from, so you can read the original yourself.

02

Real stories only

Nothing on the site is invented. If a story has no real link and image from its source, it doesn't appear.

03

Facts, not speculation

Our own videos and posts state facts from published sources. Anything that's only an artist's concept is labeled as one.

Images

Each story card shows the preview image published by that story's source, labeled "Image via" the source and linked to the original article. Page backgrounds are public-domain or Creative Commons space images, credited at the bottom of every page. Images and profile pictures in X posts come from the linked posts and accounts. Shorts are embedded from YouTube.
